# Joint Runs, 8 November 2026

The Twin, the real Guardian and the real City Brain, each from this repository, ran together as local processes for about 300 s per scenario.
No mock was involved, and no person acted: the City Brain decided alone from the Twin's readings.

## Conditions

- Not yet through the root `docker-compose.yml`.
- The Guardian used a copy of its configuration with the changes in [`partner-requests.md`](partner-requests.md).
- The City Brain ran `serve --live --no-departments` with no LLM, so its built-in rules decided, not its department agents.
- The City Brain used `guardian.adapter: v1` and `gateway: true`, so its dispatches passed through the Guardian.

## Results

| Scenario | Outcome |
|---|---|
| Pedestrian hit | Accident opened. Ambulance dispatched through the Guardian, reported `en_route`, `on_scene`, `returning`. Incident closed. |
| Car crash | The same. |
| Building fire | Fire opened in the block from its pole camera and a caller. Fire truck dispatched through the Guardian, put the fire out, reported back. Incident closed. |
| Flood | Flood opened from the trusted water level, once, with a public alert. No unit and no pump: see below. |

In the fire and flood runs no sensor was isolated, and the Guardian never had to be asked twice about a reading (0 `guardian.unavailable`).

## What was not run with the final code

The pedestrian hit and car crash ran before the sensor model and the drain zones were added, and the fire and flood before rain gauges moved back to `zone-03`.
After that change the flood was replayed through the real Guardian on simulated time, with no alerts and every water level trusted.
The City Brain took no part in that replay.
The test suite covers the same paths, without the partners' code.

## What stops a fuller flood response

The City Brain answers a flood with a public alert and a valve call.
This city has no valve, and the City Brain has no action for the Twin's drainage pump, so the water drains only if someone starts the pump.
A caller can also name a flood with the wrong category or from a dry block, and the Guardian then rightly doubts that call.
